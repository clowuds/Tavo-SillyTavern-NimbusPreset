auto_fix_high Anti-Slop All ON by default
These prompts run together as a suite. Leave all enabled unless you have a specific reason to disable one. Trope Subversion is the only one off by default.
Prohibited Words
Bans a curated list of overused, robotic, or cliché words and phrases (e.g. "absolute," "predatory," "eyes darken," "grip tighten"). Forces the AI to find more precise, varied language.
default ON
Anti-Slopinator
Bans entire categories of lazy writing: somatic clichés (shivers down spines, heart skipping), predatory tropes, texture fallacies (velvety voice), filter words ("he felt"), cosmic melodrama. Enforces precise, concrete prose instead.
default ON
Trope Subversion
Actively detects and dismantles safety scripts: moral epilogues replaced with cliffhangers, unspoken bonds replaced with suspicion, monsters that stay monsters. Use when you want deliberately unpredictable, jagged storytelling.
off by default
Anti-Robot
Bans corporate-speak ("leverage," "utilize," "validate emotions"). Forces silence to have weight, characters to have opinions, and syntax to use contractions and fragments. Keeps prose feeling human.
default ON
Anti-Echo Dialogue Seal
Prevents the AI from paraphrasing or summarizing what you just wrote back at you. Bans phrases like "hearing this," "his words registered," "the statement sank in." Response always starts from T+1 second — after your action, not during it. Disable this when Write for User is ON.
default ONdisable with Write for User
Pure-Dialogue Texture
Bans external voice tags ("he growled," "she stuttered"). Accents and speech quirks must be shown verbatim in the dialogue itself. False starts use hyphens (-), clean cutoffs use em-dashes (—). Vocal quality is conveyed through diction and punctuation only.
default ON
No Flowery
Bans poeticising instead of advancing: no "his words hit like…", no weather mirroring mood, no recapping prior turns, no flowery appearance descriptions. Every sentence must state the next sensory event as it happens.
default ON
No No-Actions
Bans describing what didn't happen ("he didn't look," "she wasn't happy"). Forces the AI to describe only what did happen — positive, concrete actions only. Exception: speech and thought.
default ON
Act Not Tell
Bans characters narrating their own habits or personality in dialogue ("I always do X," "you seem like someone who…"). Habits and emotions must be discovered through behavior — never disclosed through speech.
default ON
Sub-Text Scanner
Before every dialogue beat, the AI internally tags stakes, biases, and grudges — then infuses those undercurrents into word choice, pauses, and implications rather than stating them outright. Surface dialogue stays natural; subtext shapes it.
default ON

What's your primary use for Nimbus?
This shapes almost every other recommendation.
Step 1 of 9
Do you want the AI to write for your character?
Write as User lets you send scene prompts instead of direct actions — the AI will expand your input into full character speech and action.
Step 2 of 10
What tone or genre fits best?
Pick the closest match — you can always mix with Story Targets later.
Step 3 of 10
How do you prefer the AI to narrate?
Names use pronoun pairs: narrator voice / how your avatar is referred to. Example: They/You = narrator says "she/he/they" for the character, and "you" for you.
Step 4 of 10
How long should each AI reply be?
Dynamic variants let the AI scale within the target. Fixed variants enforce a hard cap every time.
Step 5 of 10
How autonomous should characters be?
Fully autonomous characters may surprise you, push back, or act against your wishes.
Step 6 of 10
What utilities do you want active?
These add overhead tokens but enrich the experience. Select any that apply — or just keep defaults.
Step 7 of 10
What content restrictions should apply?
Nimbus ships with Intimacy and Violence uncensored by default. Choose what you actually want active for this setup.
Step 8 of 10
Any special story ingredients?
Optional add-ons — stack as many as you like.
Step 9 of 10
Which jailbreak + reasoning mode are you using?
The Jailbreak Reinforcement prompt (top of the book) must match your chosen jailbreak. The reasoning mode controls how the AI thinks before writing. Ultra-Light Think is the default and works for most models.

Rule of thumb: Sections with Choose 1 rules will silently fight if two are enabled — the AI receives contradictory instructions and averages them into mush. Always audit one-choice sections carefully.
